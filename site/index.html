What is a node?

Here, a node is a program on your computer that organizes a task, checks information, produces a report, and keeps a record of what happened.

Do I need money or a crypto wallet?

No, not to explore this page or run the supplied examples. Optional hosted AI services may charge fees. Real token payments are a separate advanced setup using $AGIALPHA and Ethereum gas.

Can I use it without technical experience?

You can explore the example here without installing anything. Running your own node currently needs Node.js and a few terminal commands. The guide explains each step; a trusted helper can assist. There is no one-click managed account yet.

Does this page access my files?

No. The page has no upload, wallet connection, analytics, or model requests. Your language and text-size preferences may be saved in this browser. When you configure the local node, a chosen AI provider may receive task inputs; review that configuration first.

Will it earn money automatically?

No earnings are promised. Useful work, a buyer or funder, acceptance, operating costs, and a properly configured payment system all matter. Example savings and test payments are not income.

Is this the AGI Jobs or AGI Fleet installer?

No. This is the standalone $AGIALPHA node. Its default workflow does not require AGI Jobs, USDC, or OpenClaw. Historical integration guides are kept separately for reference.
